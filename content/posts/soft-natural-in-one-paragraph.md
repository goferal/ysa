If you have ever been told you have an "athletic" build but you also have curves, and nothing tailored ever sits quite right, read on.

## The one paragraph

A Soft Natural has a yang frame (a bit of width in the shoulders, a slight boniness, a relaxed openness) softened by yin flesh: curve at the bust and hips, a face with some roundness. The result is a body that wants *ease* with *shape*. Not stiff, not shapeless. Relaxed lines with a gentle waist emphasis.

## What tends to work

- Unconstructed or soft-shouldered jackets over something waist-defining
- Draped and gathered fabrics: jersey, soft knits, washed linen, silk with movement
- Wrap dresses and anything with a soft, slightly lowered waist
- Loose-but-shaped silhouettes: a relaxed top tucked into a fluid trouser
- Natural textures and slightly undone hair

## What tends to fight you

- Sharp tailoring and hard shoulders
- Stiff, crisp fabrics that hold their own shape
- Very small, fussy details and tiny prints
- Anything that fully hides the waist *or* clamps it

## The mistake we see most

Soft Naturals get told to "structure" themselves because of the shoulders, so they buy sharp blazers and feel like they are wearing a costume. The fix is usually the opposite: keep the ease, add a little waist.
