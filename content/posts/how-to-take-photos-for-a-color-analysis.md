We can do a lot with a good photo and almost nothing with a bad one, so this is worth five minutes.

## The setup

Stand facing a window in daylight, not in direct sun. Overcast is perfect. Turn off every lamp in the room, because indoor bulbs are warm and will lie to all of us.

No makeup. Hair pulled back off your face. Wear a plain white or grey top, or hold a white towel under your chin. No filters, no "auto enhance," no portrait mode.

## The photos

1. Face, straight on, eyes open, neutral expression.
2. Eyes, as close as your phone will focus. We want to see the flecks and the ring around the iris.
3. Your natural hair color, in the same light. If it is dyed, tell us what it was.

## The trick most people skip

Take one extra photo in a color you know you love, and one in a color you feel washed out in. Half the time the "love" color tells us your season faster than anything else, and the "washed out" one confirms it.
